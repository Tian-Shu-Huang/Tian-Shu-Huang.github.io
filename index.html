Tianshu Huang

I am a Research Engineer at Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL), where I work on enabling frontier models with perception and robotics abilities. Before joining Meta, I worked at Assured Robot Intelligence (ARI), which Meta acquired in May 2026. I earned a Master's degree in Computer Science from UC San Diego, where I was a graduate researcher with Xiaolong Wang. Previously, I got a Bachelor's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science (CS) from University of Michigan. I also worked as a research intern at Deep Robotics in 2024 summer.

My research interests include robot learning, humanoid control, and embodied perception.
